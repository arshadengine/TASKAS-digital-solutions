Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TASKAS Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal details when you interact with our website estimator tools or contact forms.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you directly provide to us, including your full name, email address, phone number, and any project description details submitted through our Estimator Tool or Contact form.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your details are used solely to generate preliminary estimates, follow up on project inquiries, integrate data with our internal CRM tools, and record submissions in Google Sheets for project management.

3. Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data to outside third parties.

4. Cookies

We use simple browser localStorage parameters to save and persist your website dark/light theme choice across visits.
