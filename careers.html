Graphic Designer

🎨 Design & Creative Full-time 📍 Pune / Hybrid 🎓 Freshers & Experienced

About the Role

We're looking for a creative Graphic Designer who can translate ideas into visually compelling designs across TASKAS's digital presence and client projects.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create social media posts, banners, advertisements, and promotional creatives.
  • Design graphics for websites, presentations, campaigns, and marketing materials.
  • Work with the marketing and UI/UX teams to maintain visual consistency.
  • Develop creative concepts based on project requirements.
  • Prepare designs for different digital platforms and aspect ratios.
  • Stay updated with current design trends, typography, and visual aesthetics.

Requirements

  • Strong understanding of typography, composition, spacing, and visual hierarchy.
  • Experience with tools such as Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, or equivalent.
  • Good creative and communication skills.
  • Ability to understand a brief and turn it into a finished design.
  • Portfolio demonstrating your design skills and visual craft.

Good to Have

  • Basic knowledge of UI/UX principles.
  • Motion graphics or video editing skills.
  • Experience creating social-media content and carousels.
  • Understanding of branding systems and visual identity guidelines.

Business Development Executive

📈 Business Development & Growth Full-time 📍 Pune / Hybrid 🎓 Freshers & Experienced

About the Role

We're looking for a motivated Business Development Executive who can help TASKAS identify new opportunities, connect with potential clients, and build long-term business relationships.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify and research potential clients and business opportunities across India and globally.
  • Generate and qualify outbound leads via LinkedIn, email, and discovery calls.
  • Reach out to prospective clients through professional communication channels.
  • Understand client requirements and clearly articulate them to the technical & design team.
  • Prepare proposals and coordinate with project leads on timelines and budgets.
  • Maintain strong relationships with existing and prospective clients.
  • Follow up with inbound leads and diligently track the CRM sales pipeline.
  • Contribute ideas to improve TASKAS's overarching business growth strategy.

Requirements

  • Strong verbal and written communication and interpersonal skills.
  • High confidence in pitching and communicating with decision-makers and founders.
  • Good understanding of basic sales and business development concepts.
  • Ability to research and identify high-value market opportunities.
  • Strong follow-up discipline and organizational skills.
  • Willingness to learn, adapt, and take proactive initiative.

Good to Have

  • Previous sales or client-facing business development experience.
  • Knowledge of digital services such as websites, UI/UX, software, or AI automation.
  • CRM workflow experience (HubSpot, Notion, or spreadsheets).
  • Experience in B2B service sales.

Digital Marketing Executive

🚀 Marketing & Growth Full-time 📍 Pune / Hybrid 🎓 Freshers & Experienced

About the Role

We're looking for a driven Digital Marketing Executive who can help strengthen the TASKAS brand, increase digital visibility, and build meaningful engagement across online platforms.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan and execute organic and performance digital marketing campaigns.
  • Manage social media platforms and create proactive content calendars.
  • Create and coordinate engaging social media copy and visual stories.
  • Conduct keyword and competitor research to capture search volume.
  • Assist with on-page and technical SEO activities to rank client & internal assets.
  • Monitor website analytics and campaign traffic performance.
  • Analyze engagement, conversion, and marketing ROI metrics.
  • Support paid advertising campaigns on Meta Ads and Google Ads when required.
  • Work closely with the design and business development teams to nurture leads.

Requirements

  • Solid understanding of digital marketing fundamentals and funnels.
  • In-depth knowledge of social media platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, X).
  • Basic understanding of SEO principles, keywords, and meta tags.
  • Good communication and persuasive copywriting skills.
  • Creative thinking and willingness to test and experiment with content formats.
  • Ability to analyze basic marketing and traffic data.

Good to Have

  • Familiarity with Google Analytics, Google Search Console, or SEMrush.
  • Hands-on Meta Ads Manager experience.
  • Experience with SEO auditing tools.
  • Content writing or technical blogging experience.
  • Basic knowledge of email marketing tools and automation funnels.
